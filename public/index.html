Our Story

Two Kids.
A Farmstand.
A Good Idea.

Mossy Creek Farmstand is run by William and Charlotte, who love good food, fresh air, and turning simple ideas into something special. We roast small-batch pecans in our kitchen and share them with our neighbors, friends, and anyone who loves a great pecan.

Instead of a lemonade stand this fall, we decided to try something a little different. Every bag is roasted, scooped, sealed, and stickered by hand.
